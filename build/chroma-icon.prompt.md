# 应用图标生成记录

使用内置 imagegen 工具生成。最终采用绿色圆角方形、揭开绿幕的画面、透明棋盘格与白色播放按钮。原始 PNG 保存在 chroma-icon-master.png，npm run build:icons 仅做尺寸适配与 ICNS 封装，保留透明度。

最终生成提示词：

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: one final macOS application icon for a green-screen-to-transparent-video generator. Square image. Real transparent alpha outside the icon. Make a crisp, restrained, premium geometric icon with a SINGLE SOLID EMERALD GREEN rounded-square outer tile (macOS squircle) centered with 10 percent transparent margin, no outer white border, no outer glow, NO SHADOW OUTSIDE THE TILE, no fuzzy or frosted material. Tile outer contour must be perfectly smooth, uninterrupted, sharp and clean with no detached pixels or speckles. Inside the tile, show one inset rounded video-screen rectangle. The left two-thirds of that screen are vivid green and the right third shows a clear intentional WHITE AND LIGHT GRAY transparency checkerboard with only 4 columns and 5 rows. A simple clean curved diagonal peel of the green screen reveals that checkerboard, communicating background removal. A large WHITE play triangle with subtly rounded corners sits centered in the screen, boldly readable at tiny Dock size, with a thin dark-green edge if needed for contrast. The solid emerald outer tile acts as a thick coherent frame around the internal screen. Minimal refined native macOS app icon, subtle satin green shading only inside tile, front-on orthographic view, very few shapes, vector-like clean edges, balanced symmetry. Palette emerald green, fresh green, white, pale gray only. Do not draw an Apple logo, phone, scissors, text, letters, numbers, film perforations, sparkles, floating objects, detached highlights, white bevel around outer tile, or any backdrop scene. The checkerboard is intentionally drawn INSIDE the video screen only; everywhere beyond the emerald rounded-square outer silhouette is exactly transparent.
